# Lucy · Night City Signal

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

Silver-bob netrunner with rainbow gradient tips: dual rooftop portraits, neon
cyan/magenta glass panels. A pure asset skin directory — no `package.json`, no
build step; the Skin Center is the only loader.

## What it is

- `skin.json` (manifest v2) + `skin.css` (L1 token remap, light on `:root`,
  dark on `body[data-ds-dark-theme]`) + `patches.css` (L3: the two character
  layers and the neon chrome).
- The light theme paints a daytime rooftop above the clouds; the dark theme a
  neon night skyline. The two portraits are a back view and a front view.
- The portraits were keyed locally off a flat magenta plate and despilled; the
  scenes stay opaque.
